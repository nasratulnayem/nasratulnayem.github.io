An ecommerce site has to do more than look good. Customers need to find the right product, understand it, add it to the cart, and complete the next step without confusion. The people managing the store need product, order, and content workflows that do not create unnecessary work.

## Storefront and product experience

- Shopify Liquid themes, sections, product pages, collection pages, and custom storefront improvements.
- WooCommerce product pages, gallery improvements, product video, variation handling, and custom plugin features.
- Mobile-first navigation, clear product information, and conversion-focused interface work.

## Cart, checkout, and customer flows

- Cart behaviour, product add-ons, order-related workflows, and checkout-adjacent customizations within each platform’s supported rules.
- Contact and lead forms that send the right information to email, a CRM, or an internal workflow.
- Integrations with inventory, fulfilment, analytics, marketing, and external APIs when the platform supports them.

## Product data and catalog operations

- Product imports from CSV files, APIs, and permitted source data.
- Attribute mapping, image handling, category cleanup, and repeatable catalog-update processes.
- Custom tools that reduce repetitive editing in WooCommerce or Shopify.
