Local businesses need a website that answers questions quickly, works well on a phone, and gives the right next step. The right build depends on the business, but the important pieces are usually clear information, a credible presentation, and an easy path to contact, book, order, or enquire.

## Website types I can build

### Restaurants and food businesses

Menus, featured dishes, locations, opening hours, reservations, enquiry forms, online-ordering integrations, and clear mobile calls to action.

### Dentists, clinics, and professional services

Service pages, practitioner or team information, appointment enquiries, patient or customer forms, FAQs, and a structure that makes important information easy to find.

### Schools and education providers

Programme pages, admissions information, event or enquiry forms, staff updates, downloadable resources, and content management that staff can use confidently.

### Online and service businesses

Lead-generation pages, portfolios, blogs, ecommerce, booking or quotation flows, customer intake forms, and custom integrations when a normal template is not enough.

## The technical layer

I can build these sites with WordPress, Shopify, or a custom stack, then connect the practical pieces: contact forms, email notifications, carts, product imports, content systems, APIs, dashboards, and workflow automations.
