I build the customer-facing website and the operational pieces behind it. The goal is not to add technology for its own sake; it is to make a site easier to use, easier to manage, and less dependent on repetitive manual work.

## WordPress, PHP, CSS, and JavaScript

- Custom WordPress features, plugins, theme changes, and PHP fixes.
- Clean frontend work with responsive CSS and JavaScript.
- Blog, portfolio, business-site, and landing-page builds.
- Performance-minded improvements, safer patterns, and maintainable code.
- Content tools, admin improvements, migrations, and API integrations.

## Shopify and WooCommerce

- Shopify storefront work with Liquid, sections, theme customizations, and product presentation.
- WooCommerce plugins, product-page improvements, catalog tools, and checkout-adjacent integrations.
- Cart, product, order, and form flows that match how a business actually operates.
- Product imports through CSVs, APIs, or permitted browser-assisted data collection.

## Custom websites and web apps

Some requirements do not fit a plugin. I can build focused custom tools and web applications for dashboards, forms, internal workflows, content handling, data processing, and business-specific features.

## Automation and AI integration

I use Python, JavaScript, PHP, APIs, scheduled jobs, and AI services to reduce repeat work. That can mean a small internal tool, a data-import pipeline, a support or content workflow, a reporting process, or an AI assistant connected to the systems your team already uses.
