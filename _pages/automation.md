Manual work often hides in small repeated steps: copying data between systems, preparing product records, answering the same request, assembling content, checking files, or building reports. Those steps are where a small custom tool can make a meaningful difference.

## What I automate

- Python tools for repetitive business tasks, file workflows, data cleanup, APIs, and scheduled jobs.
- WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, spreadsheet, email, and third-party API workflows.
- Permitted data collection and product-import processes from sources you control or are allowed to use.
- Internal bots and dashboards for recurring operational work.
- AI-assisted workflows for drafting, categorising, summarising, routing, and content production, with the right human checks in place.

## How AI fits in

AI is useful when it is connected to a real workflow rather than used as a novelty. I can integrate language models, image or voice generation, and agent-style task handling with your existing tools so the output goes somewhere useful: a CMS, a spreadsheet, a support queue, a product workflow, or an internal dashboard.
